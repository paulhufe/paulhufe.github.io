\opening{Dear Prof. Ringel, dear selection committee,}

I am writing to apply for the Professorship in Public Economics (W3) as advertised on the University's website with the reference STMH-317.

I completed my Ph.D. in 2021, under the guidance of Prof. Andreas Peichl, while being a part of the public economics group at LMU Munich. Subsequently, I joined the University of Bristol, where I have progressed to the role of Senior Lecturer (Associate Professor).

In recent years, I have established myself as a leading international researcher on topics related to inequality and inequality of opportunity. Within just two years of completing my Ph.D., I have published five peer-reviewed articles, including one in the Review of Economic Studies. Additionally, I have two more articles currently under review at leading journals in economics. My research has garnered significant recognition, including a dissertation prize awarded by Prof. James Heckman and the Human Capital and Economic Opportunity Global Working Group (HCEO) at the University of Chicago. Furthermore, I have been highly successful in securing external funding for my research. Most recently, I obtained a prestigious UKRI Future Leaders Fellowship with a grant totaling EUR 1,770,000 for my research project, "(Un)fair inequality in the labor market: A global study."

During my time in the UK and through research visits to distinguished institutions such as Princeton and Cornell, I have built a vast international network of co-authors. Additionally, I am actively engaged in research collaborations with researchers from various disciplines, including sociology, psychology, and education research. I look forward to contributing both my international experiences and my interdisciplinary perspective on economics to enhance the global visibility of cutting-edge research in economics at the University of Leipzig.

On a personal note, I am fully aware that pursuing a W3-level professorship at such an early stage of my career may seem unusual. However, Leipzig holds a special place in my heart, as it is my hometown, and I maintain strong personal connections there through family and friends. Returning to Leipzig has always been an aspiration of mine, and I am eager to seize the excellent opportunity presented by the advertised position.

I appreciate your consideration of my application. If you have any questions or require further information, please do not hesitate to contact me using the details provided above.
